## Important proof: site has factual integrity errors

At https://www.deerparklumber.com/Services the company's live services page contains unrelated text referencing **Carroll Construction Supply**, an **Ottumwa** service shop, and phone **(641) 455-5395**. The page's links refer to `carrollsupply.my48hour.website`. Do not conflate the services listed there with Deer Park Lumber's genuine capabilities. This is not just subjective design taste—it's a verifiable content-integrity problem.

Other observable issues:
- Generic homepage/title naming: "Home | Deer Park" instead of the actual brand + supply/location context.
- Repeated home page material blocks and crowded manufacturer information.
- Empty "Current Projects" section.
- Technical/branding residue: footer says "Powered by 48ws.com"; privacy and other footer links resolve to `b.link`.
- No clear visual separation between industrial contractor supply and homeowner/hardware audiences.

Sources: https://www.deerparklumber.com/ ; https://www.deerparklumber.com/Current-Projects ; https://www.deerparklumber.com/Services

## Key strategic angle

The owner is not buying "a better looking website." They would be buying **accurate brand representation for a 1957 family business trusted by industrial customers**. The website should communicate the same standards as the business does offline. Existing pages may actively misdirect people to another company.

The generational story is a strong conversation opener. The San Jacinto College 2025 profile describes Dean Lawther's support of workforce development, long-term Deer Park presence, and his sons' leadership. Source: https://sanjac.edu/about/news/2025/lumber-learning-why-this-business-leader-invests-san-jac-students

## The pitch

Target Warren or Jeff by calling the public main line and asking which handles company website/marketing decisions. Open with recognition of the family's history, not an insult about the website. Demonstrate **one specific error** (the unrelated Iowa supplier on the Services page), then show the independent mobile-friendly redesign. Ask for a ten-minute look, not a major commitment.

Suggested first message:

"Your family has built one of Deer Park's most established supply businesses. I was reviewing local industrial suppliers and noticed your website doesn't present the scale of what you've built—and one of the services pages appears to reference another company. I put together a cleaner concept that leads with your Ship Channel supply business, makes material inquiries simpler, and highlights your history since 1957. Would Jeff or Warren be the right person to show it to?"

## Deliverable scope

Five static pages: homepage, industrial supply, product directory with search, company story, and contact page. Mobile navigation, accessible focus styling, Google Map, phone CTAs, material-inquiry-to-email demo, and a link to the existing credit application.

**Not yet production-ready:** Independent proposal concept; pictures are externally hosted placeholders; quote form has no backend and should not accept actual inquiries before approved deployment. Obtain company authorization, brand/photo approval, and a verified quoting mailbox before going live.

## Pricing positioning (suggested, not a market fact)

Present as a B2B website overhaul, **not** a low-cost 1-page landing page. Scope can include approved content rebuild, service/category information architecture, quote capture, mobile performance, local/industrial SEO, analytics, and optional monthly maintenance. Price only after discovery on their current product catalog, account systems, and content ownership. Do not promise increased revenue without data.
