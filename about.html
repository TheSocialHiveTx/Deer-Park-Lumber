OUR STORY / 01

ONE TOWN.
THREE GENERATIONS.

1957

Deer Park Lumber grew alongside the community—and the industrial corridor that powers southeast Houston.

The story begins with W. D. Lawther and the company's early years in the 1950s. Over time, the lumber yard became a durable local institution. His son Dean played a central role in the business and Deer Park's development, and the next generation—Warren and Jeff Lawther—continued the family stewardship.

Today, the company's mix of lumber, construction materials, hardware, and industrial supply continues to serve residents and work crews across the area.
